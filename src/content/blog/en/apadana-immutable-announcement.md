Today we are proud to announce the official debut of **Apadana Linux**, the commercial enterprise operating system engineered on top of modern container-native foundations and the Parch Immutable distribution.

## Why Enterprise Needs Apadana

For decades, deploying and managing Linux desktop fleets in commercial and governmental organizations came with high maintenance friction:

- Package dependency breakage during routine rolling updates
- Configuration drift accumulating across remote employee machines
- Costly helpdesk hours spent repairing broken operating system states

Apadana eliminates these friction points entirely by isolating the core OS as a read-only verified image managed through OCI container tooling (**bootc**).

## Commercial Edition Highlights

- **Atomic Staging with Instant Rollback**: System upgrades never fail halfway. Revert to the prior verified deployment at boot in seconds.
- **Layered Application Isolation**: Seamless integration of Flatpak for desktop software, Distrobox for developer tools, and Waydroid for Android applications.
- **First-Class Persian Regionalization & KDE Plasma**: Flawless RTL ergonomics, Vazirmatn typography, and native Jalali calendar support.
- **Formal SLA & Enterprise Support**: Tailored deployment planning, security updates backporting, and dedicated SLAs.
