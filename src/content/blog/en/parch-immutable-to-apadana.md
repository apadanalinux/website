Open source software thrives on symbiotic relationships between community projects and enterprise platforms.

## Upstream & Downstream Cooperation

- **Parch Linux** is the rapid innovation engine, pioneering user-friendly modern Linux distributions with an energetic community.
- **Parch Immutable** forms the robust, bootc-native immutable base.
- **Apadana Linux** delivers the enterprise packaging, multi-year LTS maintenance, commercial SLAs, and organizational deployment frameworks.

For the latest open-source releases and community developments from our upstream counterpart, visit the [official Parch Linux Blog](https://blog.parchlinux.com).
