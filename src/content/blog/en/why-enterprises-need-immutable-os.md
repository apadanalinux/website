The largest drain on corporate IT helpdesk budgets is tracking down non-deterministic bugs across workstation operating systems. When an individual workstation develops inconsistent shared libraries or corrupted files, diagnosis takes hours of manual effort.

## Key Benefits of Immutability

1. **Deterministic Execution**: An image verified in QA behaves identically on 1,000 corporate laptops.
2. **Reduced Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR)**: Rather than hours of troubleshooting, a 10-second reboot into the prior verified state restores operations immediately.
3. **Intrinsic Rootkit Protection**: Because system binaries reside on a read-only partition, malware cannot persist in the system root.
